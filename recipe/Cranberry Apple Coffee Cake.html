Cranberry Apple Coffee Cake

.cook
#coffee cake #tested with gf flour

A soft, fluffy vegan coffee cake full of apples and cranberries and topped with a thick layer of sweet crumbs. Easy to make in one

👥 12 servings 🍽️ Breakfast, Dessert ⏱️ Prep Time: 20 minutes 📖 Modified from https://www.theppk.com/2026/09/vegan-apple-coffee-cake/

🥘 Ingredients

  • Cranberries (fresh or frozen)
    2 cups
  • Honeycrisp apples (peeled and diced into 1/2-inch pieces)
    2 cups
  • granulated sugar
    2 tablespoons
  • lemon juice
    1 tablespoon

Crumb topping

  • all-purpose flour
    2 cups
  • baking powder
    1/2 teaspoon
  • brown sugar
    1/3 cup
  • granulated sugar
    1/2 cup
  • refined coconut oil (at room temperature)
    1/2 cup

Cake

  • all-purpose flour
    2 1/2 cups
  • apple cider vinegar
    2 teaspoons
  • baking powder
    4 teaspoons
  • granulated sugar
    2/3 cup
  • olive oil
    1/2 cup
  • powdered sugar
  • pure vanilla extract
    1 tablespoon
  • salt
    1 teaspoon
  • unsweetened applesauce
    1/3 cup
  • unsweetened vegan milk
    1 1/2 cups

🍳 Cookware

  • 9 x 13-inch pan
  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a 9 x 13-inch pan .
  2. 2
    Toss the Honeycrisp apples and the Cranberries with granulated sugar and lemon juice . Set aside for about 10 minutes while you make the crumb topping and batter.
    Honeycrisp apples: 2 cups (peeled and diced into 1/2-inch pieces), Cranberries: 2 cups (fresh or frozen), granulated sugar: 2 tablespoons, lemon juice: 1 tablespoon

Crumb topping

  1. 1
    In a small mixing bowl, mix together all-purpose flour , granulated sugar , brown sugar , and baking powder . Cut in the refined coconut oil with your fingers or a pastry cutter until you have mostly large crumbs. Some sandy bits are fine.
    all-purpose flour: 2 cups, granulated sugar: 1/2 cup, brown sugar: 1/3 cup, baking powder: 1/2 teaspoon, refined coconut oil: 1/2 cup (at room temperature)

Cake

  1. 1
    In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the unsweetened vegan milk , olive oil , unsweetened applesauce , pure vanilla extract and apple cider vinegar . Sift in the all-purpose flour , granulated sugar , baking powder and salt and mix just until smooth.
    unsweetened vegan milk: 1 1/2 cups, olive oil: 1/2 cup, unsweetened applesauce: 1/3 cup, pure vanilla extract: 1 tablespoon, apple cider vinegar: 2 teaspoons, all-purpose flour: 2 1/2 cups, granulated sugar: 2/3 cup, baking powder: 4 teaspoons, salt: 1 teaspoon
  2. 2
    Fold in the fruit.
  3. 3
    Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Sprinkle the crumb topping evenly over the top and pat it down just a little.
  4. 4
    Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until a knife inserted into the center comes out clean or with just a few moist crumbs attached.
  5. 5
    Let cool for at least an hour before slicing. Dust with powdered sugar and serve!
    powdered sugar
  6. 📝

    Variations and substitutions: Can swap the all-purpose flour with gluten-free baking mix, I used King Arthur Measure for Measure.

  7. 📝
