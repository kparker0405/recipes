Peanut Butter Crisscrosses

.cook
#Cookies #Vegan
👥 24 servings 🍽️ Dessert 🌍 American 📖 Vegan Cookies Invade Your Cookie Jar

🥘 Ingredients

  • all-purpose flour
    1 1/4 cup
  • baking powder
    1/2 tsp
  • cornstarch
    2 tbsp
  • molasses
    2 tbsp
  • peanut butter
    1/2 cup
  • plant-based milk (optional)
    1 tbsp
  • salt
    1/2 tsp
  • sugar
    3/4 cup
  • two mixing bowls
  • vanilla extract
    1 tsp
  • vegan butter
    1/2 cup

🍳 Cookware

  • large mixing bowl
  • electric mixer
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 350. Lightly grease two mixing bowls . We prefer dark cookie sheets here to get the cookies browned and crispy.
    two mixing bowls
  2. 2
    In a large mixing bowl , beat together the vegan butter , peanut butter , and sugar until light and fluffy. This can take up to ⏱️ 2 minutes with an electric mixer at medium speed or ⏱️ 4 minutes just using a fork. Mix in the vanilla extract and molasses .
    vegan butter: 1/2 cup, peanut butter: 1/2 cup, sugar: 3/4 cup, vanilla extract: 1 tsp, molasses: 2 tbsp
  3. 3
    Add half the all-purpose flour along with the cornstarch , baking powder , and salt and mix well. Add the remaining flour and mix well. Add the remaining flour and mix. Use your hands at this point to really work the ingredients together. The dough should hold together when squeezed; if it does not, then add a tablespoon of plant-based milk until it does.
    all-purpose flour: 1 1/4 cup, cornstarch: 2 tbsp, baking powder: 1/2 tsp, salt: 1/2 tsp, plant-based milk: 1 tbsp
  4. 4
    Roll the dough into walnut-sized balls, flattening them a bit with the palms of your hand. The dough may crack on the edges and that is fine. Place the dough balls on the cookie sheets. Flatten them further with the bottom of a coffee mug. Use the underside of fork tines to press cross hatches into the cookies, one horizontal and one vertical.
  5. 5
    Bake the cookies for ⏱️ 10-12 minutes , until the edges are very lightly browned. Remove the cookies from the oven and let them cool on the sheets for ⏱️ 10 minutes . Use a thin, flexible spatula to transfer the cookies to wire racks to cool completely.
